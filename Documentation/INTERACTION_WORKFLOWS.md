# Interaction Workflows

## Sales demonstration

Choose a product in POS → adjust quantity/size/color → select a customer → choose a mock payment method → checkout → open generated invoice details → view invoice in Sales. Tax is demonstrated at 14%; payment is never submitted externally. Checkout also decreases the local product balance and adds an inventory movement.

## Purchase and stock demonstration

Open Suppliers → create a purchase invoice → choose supplier and product → save the sample receipt → product stock increases and a receipt movement is added. Supplier details also offer a purchase action.

## Inventory demonstration

Open Products or Inventory → select a product → inspect shared SKU, barcode, price, variants, and stock. Inventory tabs show balances and movements. Adjustments and transfers add demo movements.

## Customer demonstration

Open Customers → select a customer profile → inspect loyalty and history → open an invoice. POS can select a customer using the customer chooser; new sales update the in-memory purchase total and loyalty points.

## Administration demonstration

Open Store Administration → choose a branch → open its details or view users → inspect role/permission controls and activity log. User and settings sections remain separate modules.

## Local-only buttons

Export controls provide feedback only. Print opens browser print preview. Login accepts any non-empty values and does not authenticate. QR, barcode, discounts, and filters are simulations.
