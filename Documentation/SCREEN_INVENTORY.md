# Screen Inventory

| Screen | Route | Main content | Key links / actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Login | `#/login` | DressPOS brand, username/password demonstration, remember-me, QR branch button | Non-empty demo login opens Dashboard; forgot-password and QR actions show local feedback |
| Dashboard | `#/dashboard` | KPI cards, sales trend, category mix, top products, recent invoices, stock alerts | Opens reports, products, inventory, sales, and entity details |
| POS | `#/pos` | Product search/category selection, cards, customer selection, cart, variants, tax, discount, payment method | Checkout creates an in-memory invoice and inventory movement |
| Sales | `#/sales` | Sales chart, invoice list, status tabs, date/branch/search controls | Invoice row opens details; create invoice opens POS |
| Products | `#/products` | Product cards/catalog, category tabs, searchable table, stock/status indicators | Product details link to POS and Inventory; add-product demo form |
| Inventory | `#/inventory` | Stock balances, minimum-level warnings, stock movement tab, receipt/adjustment/transfer controls | Product detail panel; mock receipt updates stock |
| Customers | `#/customers` | Customer list, tier filters, loyalty and purchase totals | Customer profile with purchase history and invoice links |
| Suppliers | `#/suppliers` | Supplier list, contact, purchase totals, outstanding balance | Supplier profile and purchase invoice demonstration |
| Reports | `#/reports` | KPI summary, sales charts, branch comparison, top products, quick report categories | Branch/product detail links and local export feedback |
| Users | `#/users` | Users, role tabs, status filters, login activity, permission checkboxes | User detail/permission panel; add-user and role demonstration |
| Store Administration | `#/admin` | Administration overview, branches, operations modules, activity list | Branch details, users, reports, and store settings links |
| Settings | `#/settings` | Settings categories and demonstration forms | Category navigation and local save feedback |
